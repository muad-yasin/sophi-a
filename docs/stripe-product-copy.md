## Longer description (for a future product page, or Stripe's expanded description field)

Sophi-A is a desktop app (Windows, Linux) that shows eight AI-agent "seats" side by side in one
window and lets you drive them: one command-and-control chat, one advisor, three planning
modules, three building modules. The building modules and the command-and-control seat run
Claude Code directly, on your own installed, authenticated copy - Sophi-A never modifies,
wraps, or re-bundles it, and never touches your login. The command-and-control and advisor
seats can also run on OpenAI, Google Gemini, Mistral, DeepSeek, Groq, Cohere, OpenRouter,
Together, or Z.ai instead, if you'd rather point them somewhere else.

What you need: your own Claude Code installation and subscription, and/or your own API keys for
whichever providers you want to use. Sophi-A doesn't include, resell, or meter access to any of
them - it's a one-time purchase for the app itself, not a subscription, and there's nothing
ongoing to pay for.

The source is open (Apache-2.0) - this purchase buys a ready-to-run build, not the code, which
you're free to read or build yourself either way.

*(No superlatives, no "AI magic," no outcome promises - matches this workspace's own house
voice, `plan-shop.md`'s "no hype" rule. States the BYOK model plainly, which is also the
compliance-relevant fact per the Anthropic terms check.)*

## What NOT to add, and why

- No "Claude" or "Anthropic" in the product name, tagline, or any heading - even stylized or
  abbreviated. "Sophi-A" itself is clear on this point already.
- No phrase implying partnership, endorsement, or official status ("official," "in partnership
  with," "powered by Anthropic" as a badge/logo, etc.) - a plain factual sentence that it runs
  Claude Code is fine; a badge or lockup implying endorsement is not.
- No use of Anthropic's logo, wordmark styling, or color scheme to suggest affiliation.
- No claim that Sophi-A resells or bundles model access - it doesn't, and saying so would also
  misdescribe the BYOK model that keeps this compliant with Anthropic's Commercial Terms.
